now on your mouse.

Click, double-click, triple-click, hold, drag or scroll with any button, plus keyboard or button modifiers.

Install

Up and running
in a minute.

JayceMouse is free and not sold through the App Store, so macOS asks you to allow it once.

Download & move

Download JayceMouse.zip, unzip it and drag JayceMouse.app into Applications.

Download zip

Allow it to open

If macOS says it could not verify the app, click Done (not Move to Trash). Then open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. Or run:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/JayceMouse.app

Grant Accessibility

Click Grant Access in the app and switch JayceMouse on under Privacy & Security → Accessibility. The app comes alive right away.

Switched on but still “Needs access”? That happens after an update, because each build has a new signature. Remove JayceMouse from the Accessibility list with the – button and add it again, or run
tccutil reset Accessibility app.jaycemouse.JayceMouse
FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is JayceMouse really free?

Yes. It is free, has no trial and no account, and the full source code is on GitHub under the MIT license.

How do I get smooth scrolling with a mouse on Mac?

Install JayceMouse and grant it Accessibility access. Every notch of your mouse wheel then turns into a smooth, accelerating glide, like a MacBook trackpad. You can choose Low, Regular or High smoothness, or turn it off.

Can I use my mouse like a trackpad on Mac?

Yes. Hold the middle mouse button and drag to switch between Spaces, open Mission Control or show the desktop, the same things you would do with trackpad swipes. You can also map any button to Back, Forward, zoom, media keys or a keyboard shortcut.

Why does macOS say it can't verify the app?

The app is not notarized by Apple, which requires a paid developer membership. Click Done, then Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security. You only need to do this once per download.

Why does it need Accessibility access?

That permission lets JayceMouse see your mouse buttons and wheel and send the smooth scroll and shortcut events. Without it, nothing can work.

Which Macs and mice are supported?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon and Intel. It works with any USB or Bluetooth mouse. Smooth scrolling applies to notched scroll wheels; trackpads and Magic Mouse are left as they are.

Does it collect any data?

No. The app has no network code and no analytics. Your settings stay on your Mac in ~/Library/Application Support/JayceMouse.

How is it related to Mac Mouse Fix?

JayceMouse is inspired by Mac Mouse Fix by Noah Nuebling. It is an independent implementation written from scratch and shares no code or assets with it.
